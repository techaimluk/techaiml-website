Researching a used car often means piecing together information from several places. MOT results, recorded mileage, vehicle details and repair invoices can all contribute to the picture. Understanding what those records mean—and which questions remain unanswered—is another task.

An unfamiliar MOT advisory may need explaining. A recurring defect may prompt questions about maintenance. An unexpected mileage entry may require supporting documents before a buyer can draw a conclusion.

CarGPT is an AI assistant designed to help UK drivers explore these questions. Using available DVLA vehicle details and DVSA MOT history, it helps explain recorded information and prepare more focused questions about a car.

## Turning records into useful questions

An MOT history provides observations recorded at particular test dates. It does not necessarily explain what happened between tests, whether a recommended repair was completed or what condition the vehicle is in today.

AI can help users navigate that distinction. Someone reading an unfamiliar entry can ask what the wording means, how it relates to earlier results and what evidence might clarify the situation.

For example, a buyer reviewing repeated tyre-related advisories could ask CarGPT to explain the entries and suggest questions for the seller. Those questions might include when the tyres were replaced, whether invoices are available and whether their current condition has been checked.

The benefit is a more focused investigation. An explanation can help a buyer identify what to verify without suggesting that the AI has physically inspected the vehicle.

## Keeping recorded facts separate from interpretation

A useful distinction in any AI application is the difference between a source record and an interpretation of that record.

An MOT test date, result or recorded mileage comes from the available history. A possible explanation for a recurring advisory, a suggested model fault or a buying assessment involves interpretation. These carry different levels of certainty.

Consider an illustrative mileage sequence of 42,000, 50,000 and then 46,000 miles across successive tests. The decrease needs clarification, but the entries alone do not establish its cause. Test paperwork, service records and an explanation from the seller may help resolve the discrepancy.

Likewise, an advisory disappearing from a later test does not, by itself, prove that a repair was completed. The wording or the tester’s assessment may have changed. Repair invoices and an inspection provide additional evidence.

CarGPT can help users recognise these questions and understand the terminology. Important conclusions should still be checked against original records and the vehicle itself.

## How Car Report fits alongside CarGPT

CarGPT also powers Car Report, where users can enter a UK registration number to explore available MOT history, vehicle details and an AI buying assessment.

The services support two related activities. CarGPT provides a conversational way to ask questions and explore explanations. Car Report brings vehicle-specific information together in a report.

A buyer might begin by reviewing a vehicle’s recorded history, then ask what a particular advisory means or which documents to request from the seller. Another user might start with a general question about a model before researching an individual car.

Connecting those activities can make vehicle research easier to follow, particularly for people who are unfamiliar with MOT terminology or buying a used car for the first time.

## The limits matter

MOT history is useful evidence, but it is not a complete account of a vehicle’s life. It does not replace service records, establish outstanding finance or provide a complete accident history. A current MOT certificate also does not guarantee that a vehicle is safe today.

AI introduces further limitations. Answers can contain errors, source records may be incomplete or out of date, and predictions cannot establish what will happen to an individual vehicle.

Before purchasing, buyers should compare important details with official records, examine the vehicle’s documents, ask for evidence of repairs and consider an independent inspection. The government’s [MOT history service](https://www.gov.uk/check-mot-history) provides a direct source for checking recorded test information.

## A practical example of applied AI

CarGPT and Car Report illustrate a broader challenge in technology: making data available is only part of making it useful.

People also need to understand what the information describes, where uncertainty remains and what to investigate next. An effective AI application helps with that process while making its limitations visible.

For vehicle research, that means helping drivers move from unfamiliar records towards clearer explanations and better questions. Used alongside original documents and appropriate inspections, these tools can support a more informed approach to researching a used car.
